01 / Meet Tonio

More than a
conversation.

The work begins with listening.

Antonio “Tonio” Ellison, MS, CLC, brings people together around conversations that matter. He believes change often begins when someone feels safe enough to tell the truth—and someone else is ready to hear it.

Across open mics, panels, peer-support spaces and individual conversations, he helps people recognize the value in their own lived experience. His work centers on voice, self-advocacy and making room for people who too often feel unseen or unheard.

He brings that same care to every stage, workshop and community conversation.

“Your raw truth is not a burden.
It can become the beginning
of something different.”— Tonio Ellison
