About Tonio

Antonio “Tonio” Ellison, MS, CLC

My work started before there was a title for it.

I’ve spent years showing up in community spaces: hosting conversations, listening, advocating, connecting people to resources and helping create places where people feel safe enough to be honest.

Today, that work takes me into speaking, hosting, community advocacy, peer support, workshops and private conversations.

I’m especially committed to people and communities who have historically been overlooked, unheard or left out of important conversations.

People deserve
to feel seen.

My community work is rooted in making sure people who are often overlooked, unheard or underserved have somewhere to be heard and access to information that can help them advocate for themselves.

As an urban human rights and community activist, my work includes LGBTQ+ advocacy, HIV awareness and prevention, PrEP advocacy and awareness, peer support, community health conversations and connecting people with resources.

That includes making room for conversations about mental and emotional well-being. I believe advocacy starts by listening to what people are actually experiencing, then helping create access, visibility and space for those voices to matter.
