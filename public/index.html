About Tonio

More than a
conversation.

Antonio “Tonio” Ellison, MS, CLC

Tonio’s work has always been about people. The person with a story they haven’t found the words for yet. The person who’s used to listening but rarely feels heard. The person who wants to speak up for themselves and isn’t quite sure where to begin.

Across community events, open mics, panels, peer-support spaces and individual conversations, he creates room for people to show up as themselves. He listens closely, asks thoughtful questions and helps people recognize the value in their own lived experience.

His work centers on voice, expression and self-advocacy. Whether he’s holding a microphone or sitting across from someone, the intention is the same: an honest conversation, a real connection, and space to consider what comes next.

“Your raw truth is not a burden. It can become the beginning of something different.”Tonio Ellison
